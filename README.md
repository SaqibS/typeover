# TypeOver

Use your PC's keyboard to type on your phone over Bluetooth. TypeOver runs on
Windows, and your phone connects to it like a Bluetooth keyboard.

## Pair and type

1. Enable Bluetooth on your PC and phone, then start TypeOver.
2. Wait for the message `Advertising as <PC name>. Pair from your phone.`
3. Open your phone's Bluetooth settings and pair with your PC. Complete any
   pairing prompts shown by Windows or the phone.
4. Once the phone is ready to receive keystrokes, TypeOver displays
   `Click or press Enter to type to phone.`
5. Open a text field on your phone. In TypeOver, click the button or press Enter
   to begin sending keystrokes.
6. Press Esc to stop. Switching away from the TypeOver window also stops capture
   so you can use your keyboard on your PC again.

Keep TypeOver focused while typing. Closing the app stops Bluetooth advertising.

## What to expect

* You can use letters, numbers, common punctuation, arrow and navigation keys,
  F1-F12, and the numeric keypad.
* Ctrl, Shift, Alt, and Windows keys are supported. What shortcuts do depends on
  your phone and the app you're using.
* Esc is reserved for stopping capture and is not sent to the phone.
* Windows may intercept system shortcuts before TypeOver can handle them.
